Rooted in comfort, built on detail

Comfy Homes was founded in 2022 by Diana Young with a simple belief: a well-designed space can change how you feel every day. From our base in Manchester, Connecticut, we've been crafting interiors that feel both elevated and livable.

We work closely with homeowners, architects, and business owners to deliver thoughtful design — from initial concept to final styling. Every project is managed with transparency, care, and a love for the little things.

“Our goal isn't just to make a room look good — it's to make you feel at home the moment you walk in.”
